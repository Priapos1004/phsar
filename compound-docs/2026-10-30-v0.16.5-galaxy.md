# v0.16.5 — Galaxy, and search on multilingual-e5-small

## Summary

Browse gains a Galaxy tab: a 2D map of every anime, fitted from its synopsis vector, tags
and smaller blocks and grouped into named sectors and regions. Comparing encoders for the
map moved search itself to multilingual-e5-small, which re-measured every constant
calibrated on the old model.

The design study ran outside the repo on a prod restore (1,964 anime) before any code;
its scripts lived in a scratchpad, so the numbers and dead ends below are its only
durable record.

## Key decisions

### What a star is and what the fit trains on

- **One star per anime.** A media-level fit with a heavily weighted anime key was no
  better on any metric and knotted franchises tightly enough to hide everything else.
- **Fit on anime whose main story has a finished, scored media** (1,595 of 1,964); place
  the rest. Training on everything with an imputed score plus a has-score flag built a
  mixed "unrated island" (an announced isekai beside an unscored 1970s short) and
  destabilised the sectors around it.
- **Blocks scaled to unit mean squared pairwise distance, times √weight**, so squared
  distance is a weighted sum of the blocks'. Weights ("balanced", which beat desc-heavy
  and genre-heavy on neighbour tag Jaccard and a taste judge): desc 4, tags 4 (genres 2 +
  themes 2 in the study, one set below), demographics 0.7, source 0.5, format 0.5, age 0.3,
  era 0.3, studios 0.3, quality 0.5.
  Equal weights are not equal influence — synopsis distances bunch, so tags order 39% of a
  star's 50 nearest candidates against the synopsis' 21%. Kept as is (user's call).
- **Studios: top 25 one-hot plus big/mid/small buckets** beat raw one-hot (hundreds of
  near-empty columns) and buckets alone. A rarity-weighted studio set changed 21% of
  neighbour lists with no measurable gain.
- **Quality (score + log votes) stays at 0.5**: higher weights split sectors by score
  before content.
- **The era has a floor (1970) and no ceiling.** Every block is rescaled by its own spread,
  so a range's divisor cancels and a ceiling only clips — a 2027 one would have read every
  later anime as 2027. Tying it to the date instead would still move: it puts the clock in
  the fit and changes `vectors_key` every year, which refits the map cold. Dropping the
  ceiling cost one cold refit.

### The pipeline

UMAP to 10-D (n_neighbors 15, min_dist 0) → HDBSCAN sectors on the 10-D → Ward regions per
sector on [10-D, z(score), z(era)] → a 2-D UMAP fitted on the 10-D.

- HDBSCAN on the raw ~500-d vectors called 73–100% of stars noise; on UMAP-10 it works.
  `excess of mass` collapses into one sector unless `max_cluster_size` caps it (n/6, with
  `min_cluster_size` 25, gives ~16 sectors).
- Ward per sector with score and era as extra dimensions gives the splits the user asked
  for ("cheat-skill isekai, the sloppy ones" vs the acclaimed ones); HDBSCAN `leaf` regions
  were fragmentary and never split by score.
- 2-D from the 10-D beat 2-D from the raw vectors and t-SNE: trustworthiness@10 0.94 and
  sector contiguity 0.96 against t-SNE's 0.91 / 0.88, and t-SNE cannot place new points.
- **More, smaller sectors are less stable, not more.** Swept on three seeds: today's
  settings give seed ARI 0.85; min size 15 gives 20–35 sectors at ARI 0.76–0.83; coarser
  (40 / 10) gives 10 sectors at 0.95. Smaller sectors are more homogeneous but every extra
  boundary is one more a rerun draws differently. Regions are the finer layer; small tight
  genres (BL, Josei, villainess LN, Ghibli) stay regions.
- **Cost:** a fit takes ~14 s on 2 threads at ~520 MB peak, which is why it runs in a child
  process.

### Distance: missing values are unknown, not zero

Decomposing a suspiciously close pair showed themes contributing 0 because **neither had
a theme**: 34% of anime carry no theme, and an empty block made every two of them
identical there. Unscored anime took the median score and zero votes, and 92% of their
neighbours were unscored too.

- **Tags are one rarity-weighted set with a pseudo-count** (the user's design):
  similarity = shared / √((n₁ + α)(n₂ + α)), α = 2, each tag weighted by −ln share, so
  "4 of 5 shared" beats "2 of 2" and Idols + Music outweighs Action + Comedy. Full-strength
  rarity beat half strength on neighbours and on meaning.
- **One rule for every other missing value**: a value known to degree c shrinks toward the
  training mean by √c and carries (1 − c) of the spread as uncertainty, so absent = mean
  plus the whole spread. Distance is d² = |u₁ − u₂|² + o₁ + o₂, exact — UMAP 0.5.12 fits
  and transforms from a precomputed matrix, and the neighbours query orders by
  `(features <-> q)² + uncertainty`.
- **An empty synopsis was the real pull.** The stored vector then embeds the titles alone,
  short texts sit together, and 88% of such an anime's neighbours had none either. Counted
  by length (c = L / (L + 50)) → 45%; half-lengths 30 / 50 / 70 were near-identical
  (synopsis agreement 83.4 / 83.1 / 82.9), larger ones shift influence from synopsis to
  tags for everyone. Shrunk toward the mean embedding, not zero: random synopses already
  sit at cosine ~0.82, so a zero-shrunk one would land past every typical pair.
- **Demographics stay zero**: their absence is structural (12% of manga adaptations lack
  one, 79–100% of everything else), and treating it as unknown cost the map structure
  (noise 19% → 28%).
- Net, on the real catalogue: 0–1-badge neighbours 19.7% → 12.3%, themed anime with a
  theme-less neighbour 27% → 15%, empty-synopsis anime's empty neighbours 88% → 45%.

### Placement, between, and sector colour

- **Each anime is placed alone** (~7 ms, unpickle included). UMAP's `transform` optimises
  a batch together, and a weakly evidenced anime — nearly equidistant from everything —
  landed differently depending on its batch (7 of 80 moved over 3 map units, max 14).
- **"Between" is the GLOSH outlier score's tail** — at most 10% of training anime (strict
  `>`), placed anime past the same score. Compared on five runs at equal share, it flagged
  the most consistent set refit to refit (Jaccard 0.56 vs the noise label's 0.41, low top
  membership 0.36, near-tie 0.30). Sector churn (15–20% between runs) is spread over the
  map, so no reading predicts it.
- **Noise takes its sector by a 10-nearest vote in 10-D**, not the membership argmax: the
  argmax sent 18.4% of placed stars into another sector's colour, often a far one (vote:
  2.2%).

### Refits and names

- **Refit quarterly, warm-started** from the previous coordinates. Backtests holding out
  the newest month to two years and placing them: sector ARI to the full fit 0.76–0.83 up
  to a year, 0.71 at two (seed to seed 0.85–0.86); placed anime sat between regions 26–38%
  of the time and matched their fitted sector about half the time, so placing forever
  drifts. A cold refit moves the median star 32% of the view, a warm one 7% (both boxed to
  the same frame, since the UI fits the map to the viewport and a whole-map shift is
  invisible).
- **One rule over stored state decides a rebuild** (`ensure_map`). The first cut triggered
  from a calendar check on the 15th and from a boot flag; each lost its trigger for good
  when one moment was missed.
- **Warm only when the vectors match** (`vectors_key` = `FEATURES` plus a hash of every
  tunable a vector reads, and the same model). Comparing all of `built_from` was too broad
  (a numpy bump would reshuffle the map) and too narrow (a retuned weight would warm-start
  across it — the between share jumped 11% → 31% on exactly that).
- **Names carry by trained-member overlap, floor 0.4 Jaccard**, since sector ids churn
  between fits (above). Carrying the study's 71 hand names onto the app's fit:
  0.3 → 63, **0.4 → 59**, 0.5 → 50, 0.6 → 47; the names 0.4 adds over 0.5 fit their groups.
- **Auto names from training anime only**: a tag at ≥ 40% share and ≥ 1.6× lift over the
  reference (the catalogue for a sector, its sector for a region), the source at ≥ .55 and
  1.4×, a studio at ≥ .3 and 2×, ±0.35 median score for acclaimed / low-rated, and 6 years
  older or 2 newer for classic / recent. The tag floor was 45% and missed Kids at 44%.
  Counting placed anime diluted shares (54% of them have no theme vs 30%) and pushed
  regions back to "mixed". Hand names reach prod through the admin rename endpoint, not a
  seed: a fresh database must work on auto names alone.
- **A dropped hand name is listed with where its members went** (`names_dropped`, share of
  old trained members ≥ 10%). Jaccard punished a big new group — a clean split into two
  40-star groups scored 0.27 on both halves and showed one; the share reads a split as its
  halves. 10% rather than 25% because a dissolved group's pieces are what a rename needs.
- **A group's name anchor is computed in the fit** (`ANCHOR_RADIUS`), not in the browser,
  which only settles collisions — so the colouring and the names read one position.

### Serving and the tab

- **Neighbours are served on demand** from stored feature vectors, not inlined in the map
  payload: fresh after each placement, and the query the recommender needs.
- **Rows, not columns, for `/browse/galaxy`**: 213 KB gzipped against 163 KB columnar,
  since UUIDs, titles and cover URLs dominate either way and the tab loads it once.
- **Neighbour reasons ranked by chance** in the backend (shares the map doesn't carry; the
  recommender will want the same), after "Top 46% synopsis" showed up as a reason.
- **Filters via a sibling `GET /search/anime/uuids`**, not a flag that would change
  `/search/anime`'s typed response. No query box: a description query lights a ranked list
  cut at a margin, an edge the map can't show. Filtered-out stars sit in a silent series (they never steal hover) and
  shape only the nebula's veil (in the haze they fogged the map grey).
- **MAL score colours by "Top N%" rank** — the absolute ramp was nearly one shade over the
  map. Lit stars with no value take a muted teal ≥ 15 OKLab ΔE from the gold, the theme
  primaries and the ramp's ends.
- **The nebula is one tone-mapped density image per view**: 1,964 additive radial
  gradients lagged and read as harsh neon.
- **`/ratings/coverage` sends the rated state only**; the tier is derived in the frontend
  beside the styles it picks between, rather than a derived field sent beside its source.

### Search on multilingual-e5-small

Compared inside the galaxy pipeline, then on search (30 targeted queries in EN / JA /
JA-title / DE):

| Model | MRR EN | JA | JA title | DE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2 | 0.50 | 0.42 | 0.07 | 0.23 |
| multilingual-e5-small | 0.73 | 0.85 | 0.60 | 0.44 |

Same size (118M, 384-d), so no schema change and no extra RAM, and it gave the most stable
galaxy. Keyword precision is about equal. mxbai-embed-large is English-only (Japanese
collapses); e5-large and bge-m3 are too large for the 4 GB VM. e5 needs `query: ` /
`passage: ` prefixes and its distances are compressed, so every calibrated constant was
re-measured. The procedures and scripts are in `.claude/skills/remeasure-embeddings`;
this run's numbers are below, on the local prod restore re-embedded by the app's own pass
(1,964 anime / 6,724 media / 345 notes in 236 s on the dev Mac).

**`SEMANTIC_MARGIN` = 0.06, one value for both populations.** Semantic-only hits admitted:

| Query set | 0.04 | 0.05 | **0.06** | 0.07 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| description, worst of names (shinji, izumi, rintaro, okabe) + nonsense (xyzzy, spreadsheet, tax audit, asdf qwer) — rule: ≤ 2 | 32 | 7 | **1** | 0 |
| description: giant robots / boy who sees ghosts / detective solves murders / friends start a band | 60 / 60 / 60 / 60 | 38 / 38 / 60 / 60 | **11 / 15 / 42 / 26** | 2 / 5 / 16 / 7 |
| notes, worst nonsense (xyzzy, spreadsheet, tax audit, submarine, pizza recipe, good, nice) — rule: 0 | 2 | 1 | **0** | 0 |
| notes: calm and cozy / hilarious / dull and tedious / detective story | 10 / 8 / 12 / 21 | 4 / 6 / 7 / 5 | **1 / 4 / 6 / 0** | 1 / 2 / 1 / 0 |

Description survivors at 0.06 are relevant. Notes at 0.05 would recover all four relaxing
notes for "calm and cozy", but "detective story" then admits five notes that only say
"story". The name/nonsense list is a reconstruction — the v0.16.0 one was never written
down in full; this one is.

**`DESC_THRESHOLD` = 0.93** (merge detection's title + description rule; the old 0.85 had
no written procedure).
- Negatives: every catalogue pair the rule decides (studio overlap, title score in
  [0.5, 0.85)) — 245 pairs, distinct by the admin's past curation. Under e5 the nearest
  sits at 0.922; franchise siblings at 0.90–0.92; two unrelated isekai at 0.889.
- Positives, the shape of a double save: main-story media pairs inside one anime in the
  same band — 972 pairs. e5 recall 0.91 / 0.70 / 0.43 / 0.21 / **0.13** / 0.08 at 0.86 /
  0.88 / 0.90 / 0.92 / **0.93** / 0.94; the old model at 0.85 recalled 0.09.
- 0.93 flags no negative and slightly out-recalls the old threshold. The two pairs the
  admin dismissed sit at 0.917 and 0.888. Title-alone and relation links carry most
  detections, so this rule is tuned for precision.

**Encode length stays 512 tokens** (MiniLM stopped at 128): the search study validated e5
at 512 and a cap would void the calibration. At 2 threads, 150 real descriptions: 19.3 ms
mean on MiniLM, 23.8 ms on e5; the longest synopsis 17.9 vs 45.3 ms. The VM re-embed moves
to ~6–11 min.

**Discover's description rows** were checked under e5: under the search's own literal test
there is one semantic-only hit across all twelve rows, so the model moved none of them.
The rows' overlap is literal (isekai/reborn share 39 anime; a long franchise matches through
one movie): the ROADMAP's "To study" carries both.

**The re-embed window** (mixed vector spaces for a few minutes after deploy) is accepted
pre-1.0. Rolling back to v0.16.4 is not
automatic — it predates the boot probe and re-embeds only with
`EMBEDDING_REEMBED_ON_STARTUP=true`.

**A probe, not a flag, and run after a restore too.** One stored row re-encoded at boot
replaces the env flag someone had to remember. This release's first boot takes a dump of
the old model's vectors — its backup self-heal runs before the re-embed — so a restore runs
the same pass as boot ([search](../docs/features/search.md#embeddings)).

### Your own score as a filter

The anime grain reads the plain mean the rating card shows, not MAL's main-story weighted
mean, so the filter keeps what the card shows; the media grain reads the media's own
rating.

### The weekly sweep window: 5 → 3 years

On prod the update sweep averaged ≈ 483 media a night against a cap of 500, ≈ 238 of them
from the weekly cycle. From 60 days of `media_changes` on finished main entries ("real
change" = anything but votes, score drift under 0.05, cover extension flips):

| age | real change / 60 d | score moved ≥ 0.05 |
|---|---|---|
| 0–0.5 y | 56% | 38% |
| 0.5–1 y | 16% | 3.7% |
| 1–3 y | 6–9% | 0–2.5% |
| 3–5 y | 1.4–3.8% | 0% |

Not 1–2 years: the relations probe runs only for anime with a media due that night, so the
window also paces sequel discovery — it found three new sequels on franchises 2.0–2.4 years
old and only back-catalogue filler at 3–5. ≈ 60 fewer media a night on prod. It stays a
module constant: the data argued one new value for every deployment, not a per-deploy knob.

### Inert controls

- **One dim, opacity-40, as a base style on `[aria-disabled="true"]`**, and hover skipped
  app-wide by overriding Tailwind's `hover` variant. A per-call-site class let a new inert
  control lose hover but not dim. The guard sits in `:where()` so hover's specificity is
  unchanged; a bare `:not()` would have raised every hover utility over the variants it
  competes with by order.

## Failed approaches

- **Filling missing values with the mean**: no change — every untagged anime gets the same
  vector, which acts as a theme of its own.
- **Filling from similar anime** (kNN): weak (5-fold R² score .23, age .32, watch time
  .04) and it counts the predictor twice — two unscored anime filled from the same genres
  come out alike *because* they share genres, which the tag block already rewards.
- **Genres + themes as a plain cosine set**: short lists still matched exactly; the pull
  only weakened.
- **A per-anime random direction** approximating the uncertainty term: worked, but the
  exact form cost little once UMAP took a precomputed matrix.
- **Placing at the transform's starting point** (deterministic): half the placed anime then
  sat between regions, and a third changed sector. **Leaving a row's own uncertainty out of
  UMAP's input**: worse (max movement 23).
- **Discover's query words**: "overpowered" for strongest and "aveng" / "vengeance" for
  revenge each dropped most of the on-topic shows; "apocalyp" (9 → 15) added two real
  post-apocalyptic shows and franchise noise, and the user kept "apocalypse".
- **ECharts' `shiftY`** for spreading names: it is a one-column algorithm (sorts every
  label by y and pushes each below the last, whatever its x), so names side by side pushed
  each other off their clusters. Replaced by `spreadLabels`.

## Gotchas & learnings

- **HDBSCAN's `max_cluster_size` (n/6)** means a synthetic catalogue of a few big clusters
  finds no sectors — every cluster is over the cap. The tests use eight small clusters, and
  leave no star as noise, so the noise vote is unit-tested instead.
- **A hand-written |a|² + |b|² − 2ab expansion cancelled to zero** on the synthetic
  catalogue, where one block had no spread and its near-zero scale blew a column up. Use
  scipy's `cdist`; leave a zero-spread block unscaled. Changing the float bits this way also
  changed the sector count under the same seed (15 → 13).
- **"At most 10% between" needs `np.quantile(..., method="higher")`.** Strictly past an
  interpolated quantile takes ⌈0.1·n⌉ stars, one over the share whenever 0.1·n isn't whole;
  a layout that happened to dodge it hid the bug until a float-bit change moved the stars.
- **A precomputed UMAP keeps its whole training distance matrix** (quadratic), of which
  `transform` reads only the row count. Cut before pickling: 4.3 MB.
- **`alembic revision --autogenerate` refuses to run while an enum's labels differ**: hold
  the new label back, autogenerate the tables, hand-write the `ALTER TYPE`
  ([database rules](../.claude/rules/database.md)); the downgrade's re-cast is what taught
  the partial-index rule there.
- **ECharts:** `labelLayout` is handed the label's box where the last redraw left it, so a
  shift computed from `labelRect` undoes itself — place absolutely from the anchor.
  `image://` symbols stretch covers and `symbolKeepAspect` holds only for the first load —
  crop to 2:3 on a canvas and draw through a custom series. The inside dataZoom zooms each
  axis separately and overruns the edge — reset both windows to the wider span after every
  zoom. `hideOverlap` ranks by symbol area, then by an internal element order that isn't
  the data order — a sub-pixel `symbolSize` carries the rank. Measure label sizes with
  `echarts.graphic.Text`, passing the outline as `stroke` / `lineWidth`. Reading the zoom
  through `getOption()` and redrawing the overview every zoom frame lagged; read the
  model's range and debounce. Hot reload never swaps the chart's callbacks; reload the page
  to see a layout change.
- **`?focus=` on `/browse` has three readers** (News', Discover's, the Galaxy's), which
  coexist because a bare uuid matches no carousel. SvelteKit's `replaceState` leaves
  `page.url` as it was, so the Galaxy still reads it after a row strips it.
- **A shadcn `Button` with `href` and `disabled`** renders `<a aria-disabled>`, which the
  base style dims; its `disabled:` utilities never reach an `<a>`.

## Future work / debt

- **Routing `?focus=` once from the page to the active tab**, when a fourth reader appears:
  children mount before the page's `onMount`, so it needs `consumeFocus` split into a read
  and a strip.
- **Neighbour reasons' `trait_counts`** cost ~27 ms warm per pick; recount at fit time
  beside the percentiles once the recommender asks per item.
- **Merge detection's title + description rule** re-measures titles when a description is
  empty. 3 of 245 band pairs involve one, none near 0.93; a `description != ""` guard
  matching search's would close it if that changes.
- **The re-embed's completion probe** reads a catalogue row that other writers can also
  re-encode under a new encoder (`embeddings_are_stale` says when), so a skipped or
  interrupted pass can read as done. A one-row sentinel would close it: `encode_description`
  of a fixed text, written only once the whole pass commits, and cleared by a restore before
  `pg_restore` so a dump without one reads stale.
