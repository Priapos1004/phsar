# v0.15.3 — quality-of-life upgrades & little fixes

## Summary
Seven items, three of them bugs earlier releases left behind. Jobs Log: `upcoming_sweep`
rows rendered a blank Detail cell, backup rows didn't show the dump size, and the kind
label was overlong. Ratings statistics gained **season** and **age-rating** breakdowns
(subtab renamed Categories) with click-through to the filtered grid, and the cumulative
watch-time chart now reads its own window. The sweep's long tail gained a per-row
180-day window for media over a decade old. Backups record the Alembic revision they
carry, and a restart with nothing restorable takes a dump immediately. No migration —
every new field is Pydantic-only or lives in the existing per-dump JSON sidecar.

## Key decisions (rationale not obvious from the code or docs)
- **The archival sweep cadence is a per-row WINDOW on one atom, not a fifth tier.**
  `due_long_tail` compares `last_checked` against a `CASE` interval (180 days once
  `aired_from` is past the archival cutoff, else 90). A fifth OR-branch was written first
  and rejected: `due_long_tail` is unconditional, so anything stale 180 days is already
  stale 90 — the new branch is dead code unless tier 4 is narrowed by `not_(archival)`,
  and that narrowing is a coupling nobody will remember. The window form also makes NULL
  correct *for free* (an undated media fails the `WHEN`, lands on the 90-day `ELSE`),
  which deleted a guard, its comment, and the reasoning behind both.
- **`integrity` stays a pure file-corruption verdict; the composite is a separate field.**
  `apply_retention` pins the most-recent-`ok` dump as its archival anchor, so folding a
  schema verdict into `integrity` would leave retention anchorless *immediately after
  every migration* — exactly when the install is most exposed. The third option (a
  sibling derived field) is the right one; "compose it in the UI" was a false third.
- **`unknown` is deliberately not `ok`.** A dump with no recorded revision might restore
  cleanly, but nothing on disk says so. Advertising it as `ok` is advertising a backup you
  can't vouch for. This is also what makes the card and the self-heal agree.
- **`resetKey` belongs on `EChart`, not `{#key}` in the consumer.** The behaviour being
  overridden (`setOption(…, notMerge)`) is the wrapper's decision, and a consumer remount
  additionally re-pays `echarts.init` and blanks the plot for the initial-measurement tick.
- **Season/age labels drill into your own ratings; studio labels leave for search.** Not an
  inconsistency — they answer opposite questions ("what did I rate in Fall 2025" vs "what
  else did this studio make"). Genre stays inert because it has no second question.
- **Categorical bucket keys are the raw filter value (`"17"`, not `"R-17+"`).** The
  click-through writes the key straight into `ratingsFilter`; a display-label→filter-value
  reverse lookup breaks silently the first time a label is reworded.
- **The backup self-heal runs FIRST among the post-yield passes.** All of them are
  idempotent derived-data repairs that re-run every boot, so a dump taken before them
  loses nothing recoverable — restore it, restart, they re-derive. A dump taken *after*
  them could not recover from a backfill that damaged data.
- **`JOBS_SWEEP_MAX_PER_RUN` stays at 500.** Prod is over-subscribed (~652 due/night at
  the time of writing) but `ORDER BY last_checked_at ASC` self-balances: a media that
  misses a night is staler the next and outranks whatever was refreshed. The cap spreads
  load fairly rather than starving a tier — do not "fix" it.

## Failed approaches (all of these were built before being reverted)
- **The animation bug got the wrong diagnosis first, and the fix exposed a second bug.**
  I blamed ECharts index-diffing between datasets; the correct cause was `resize()`
  finishing in-flight animations, fired by the `ResizeObserver` *after* the option
  `$effect` because the chart's height tracks its row count. Fixing that then made the
  `notMerge` **exit** tween visible — old bars animating away over the new ones. Two
  mechanisms, two layers: the resize guard is in `EChart`, the `resetKey` opt-in is what
  the consumer passes. **Learning: an intermittent animation is usually cancellation, not
  a wrong tween — and removing a mask reveals whatever it was masking.**
- **Keying the remount on all three controls.** Over-generalized from "dim swap" to
  "any control change", which forced a restart-from-zero on re-sorts where an in-place
  tween is the better affordance. Scoped back to `dim`.
- **A frontend-composed restorability verdict — shipped in the branch, caught by review.**
  `dumpStatus` returned `'ok'` for `schema_current === null` while `ensure_up_to_date_backup`
  treated the same row as unrestorable. On the first boot after this feature shipped, the
  card would have green-lit every legacy dump while the backend had just decided nothing
  on disk was restorable. **Learning: when two consumers answer the same question, the
  answer is a field, not a helper each of them calls.** Neither the tests nor I caught
  this; the altitude review did, by comparing the two predicates directly.
- **`EXISTS(aged) AND NOT EXISTS(newer)` for the anime-grain archival roll-up.** Correct,
  but the planner hashed both subplans over all of `media`: **+68% on the Overview card's
  query** (14.9 → 25.1 ms, +737 buffers). A correlated `MAX(aired_from) < cutoff` is
  provably identical on every shape and costs +1.5 ms. **Learning: `MAX` ignoring NULLs is
  what makes the ∀ roll-up NULL-safe without a guard** — the two-EXISTS form needed four
  lines of comment defending against a vacuous `NOT EXISTS`.
- **A `title` prop on `TagBarLabel` that no caller ever passed** — added for a case the
  formatter already handled before the prop was reached.
- **A DOM-driven test for the "By restorability" sort.** The shadcn Select can't be driven
  in jsdom (`getByRole('combobox')` finds nothing). Extracting the comparator to a util and
  testing it directly is better anyway — the component test can't assert ordering without
  also asserting the dropdown's internals.
- **A test assertion that was simply wrong**: `paddedAxisMin({min: 5, max: 5}) === 0`. The
  pad is 0.01, so it returns 4.99. Written from the docstring rather than the arithmetic.

## Gotchas & learnings
- **SQL `NOT NULL` is `NULL`.** Negating a nullable comparison to narrow a branch drops the
  NULL rows out of *both* branches. Here that would have meant undated media never being
  refreshed again — silent, permanent, and invisible in any test that didn't specifically
  seed a NULL. The eventual fix removed the negation entirely rather than guarding it.
- **`_seed_anime` defaults `aired_from=None`**, so a new "undated media" test was
  byte-identical to the existing long-tail test — one behaviour pinned twice, coupled by a
  cross-reference comment. The existing test now pins an explicitly *dated* row.
- **`cumulativeWatchTime` filtered by cutoff *after* accumulating**, so clipping the x-axis
  also clipped the visible history's start: the series began at the first in-window rating
  with no leading plateau. A window containing no ratings drew nothing at all — and with
  `1m` as the default range, that was anyone who hadn't rated in a month.
- **A component-local function is unreachable from tests, which is how it drifts.**
  `payloadSummary` had no coverage for either sweep kind, which is exactly why the
  `seasonal_sweep` literal survived the `upcoming_sweep` release. Extracting it to
  `jobSummary.ts` produced 16 tests and immediately documented three schema eras.
- **Tests never boot the lifespan** (`tests/routers/conftest.py` uses `ASGITransport`
  without a `LifespanManager`), so the startup self-heal adds no `pg_dump` to the suite.
  Worth knowing before adding anything else to `_post_yield_backfills`.
- **Monkeypatched stubs encode the return shape.** Widening `_compute_content_hash` to
  `(hash, revision)` touched six stubs across one file — the third lockstep edit to those
  same lines.
- **`_rebuild_meta` uses the cheap TOC check, not the hash pass**, so it cannot recover the
  revision — an orphan-rebuilt sidecar reads `unknown` forever. That mattered more once
  `unknown` started driving behaviour.
- **Svelte 5 warns on `let x = someProp`** (non-reactive capture) even when a snapshot is
  what you want. A `Symbol` sentinel expresses "no value applied yet" without the warning
  and keeps `undefined` usable as a legitimate prop value.
- **Snapshot statistics rot in comments.** "The long cycle is ~73% of the catalog" was
  true the day it was measured. Restated structurally: it's the *terminal* bucket every
  media drains into and never leaves, so it's the only tier whose cost grows without bound.

## Open items — decide before merge
- **`_rebuild_meta` can't recover `alembic_revision`.** An orphaned dump's rebuilt sidecar
  reads `unknown` permanently, and `unknown` now decides whether the self-heal fires. A
  targeted `pg_restore --data-only -t alembic_version -f -` decompresses one tiny block —
  cheap enough that the "one pass or nothing" trade-off doesn't actually apply.
- **The restore endpoint doesn't gate on `status`.** The confirm dialog surfaces the verdict
  now, but `POST /admin/backups/{filename}/restore` will happily roll the schema back. With
  the composite on the DTO, requiring an explicit acknowledgement for a non-`ok` dump is
  cheap. (Deliberately left restorable — rolling back is a legitimate admin choice.)
- **A 3 ms/night micro-fix was identified and NOT applied**: guarding the
  `COPY public.alembic_version` detection with `if revision is None` short-circuits the
  `startswith` for the rest of the stream (`alembic_version` sorts first among `public`
  tables). A guard *inside* the capture branch was removed as unreachable — correct — but
  the useful guard is on the detection side and is still absent.
- **`_compute_content_hash` no longer matches its name or its job** (it returns a tuple and
  extracts schema metadata). `_scan_dump(path) -> DumpScan` NamedTuple would stop the next
  extraction widening a tuple that six test stubs hand-roll.
- **`SweepTiersCard`'s key union re-lists `AdminSweepTierBreakdown`'s keys.** Deriving it
  (`Exclude<keyof …, 'stabilizing_by_check'>`) would make a bucket missing from `ROWS` a
  type error instead of the silent sum-invariant break the card's own comment warns about.
- **The Overview count card is still uncached** and now runs a correlated MAX + MIN + two
  EXISTS per anime. v0.14.8 already flagged caching; this release made it cheaper than the
  first draft but not cheaper than before.
- **`EXPLAIN` the count-card query at prod scale.** Every measurement here is against the
  dev catalog (1701 media / 279 anime); prod is ~2.5× that and the two-EXISTS gap scaled
  roughly linearly. The *selection* query was measured as plan-identical and needs nothing.
- **The reuse review never reported for items 1–5** (commits `7cc1b57`…`bb376cd`). That
  angle is uncovered for the jobs-log, Categories and chart work — worth a pass at PR time.

## Manual verification (done in-browser during the session)
Every user-facing surface in this branch was exercised by hand and behaves as intended:
- the Categories chart — a dimension swap grows the bars in from zero with no residue of
  the previous dimension, and a re-sort tweens in place without restarting;
- season and age labels land on `/ratings?tab=ratings` with the right filter and the grid
  view active;
- cumulative watch time at the default `1m` range — readable slope, flat left edge, and a
  plateau rather than an empty chart for a month with no ratings;
- the Backups card — `ok` / `schema outdated` / `unknown` pills, the live-revision
  reference in the controls row, and the per-row revision beside the size;
- the admin Overview SweepTiersCard at both grains, five rows summing to 100%.

Three of these were found *by* that hand-checking rather than confirmed by it — the ghost
bars, the cumulative chart's missing left-edge plateau, and the calendar-month vs 30-day
window question all came from the browser, not from the tests. Worth remembering how much
of this batch the automated suite could not have caught: the tests pin the maths and the
formatters, not whether an animation plays.

Still with no automated coverage (deliberately, not by oversight): the ECharts render path
has none — jsdom has no canvas, and the two animation bugs here were both invisible to
`svelte-check` and vitest.

Operator notes for the deploy: the **first boot takes one extra backup** (no dump on disk
records a revision yet, so nothing is `ok`) — expected, one-time. The first sweep after
deploy moves ~46% of media onto the 180-day window, which is strictly *less* work, so
there is no herd to plan for.
