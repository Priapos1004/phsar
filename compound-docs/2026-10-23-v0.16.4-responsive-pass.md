# v0.16.4 — responsive pass, touch, genre groups

## Summary

Every page was walked from 320 to 1920 with the user finding the issues live — the
procedure is now the `/ui-pass` skill. Most fixes landed once, in a primitive or a `cls`
token, rather than per page. Tooltips open on a tap. Search's genre filter became one
level of groups, which also let Discover rows exclude the genres that made them repeat
each other.

## Key decisions

### Layout

- **Long words break globally.** `body { overflow-wrap: break-word }` won over per-site
  `wrap-anywhere`, which every author had to remember; `anywhere` globally lost because it
  lowers min-content and would collapse auto-sized table columns.
- **Below 360, `cls.container` drops its gutter, not `main`.** Halving `main`'s gutter
  first left every container-less page 8px from the edge, and adding the 8px back on each
  wrapper only cancelled the halving.
- **The ratings and watchlist tables scroll sideways rather than drop columns.** Hiding
  MAL and Rated put their sorts out of reach without rotating the phone. A sticky title
  column was rejected: the card is translucent glass, and a sticky cell needs an opaque
  background. Sideways-scrolling the Categories chart lost — it hides the bar ends the
  comparison lives in — so it takes a narrow label column on a phone. A content-sized one
  made the chart jump whenever the direction flip swapped the names.
- **Search stays two columns at 1920**; every anime page widens to 7xl from 2xl together.
  Three columns lost: the list is ranked and a wider zigzag loses the order, the
  text-dense card reads best at ~450–650px, and results get compared side by side.
  Many-column poster browsing already exists on Browse and the see-all pages.
- **See-all toggles share the row's width on a phone** (`stretchOnPhone`). A 2×3 grid
  lost: it breaks a percentage scale over two lines.
- **The bell stays at every width** — below 360 the navbar takes the page's `px-2`.
  Hiding it would have meant a job list on every page that starts a job.
- **One quality colour scale** (`QUALITY_COLORS`) for scores and attribute bars: the
  histogram's red, yellow, green, purple — the user's pick. Rose/orange/emerald/blue and a
  single-hue ramp (too close) lost. A bar's colour is its value's place in the option
  list; counting the values present made "Rare" change colour between anime. Yellow is
  1.72:1 on the `bg-muted` track and 2.1:1 as text on white, legal only because marks
  carry text labels and segments keep their 2px gaps — keep both.

### Search cache

- **A cached media search drops on every write.** Its hits carry the caller's own
  `is_rated`, so it counts as personal whatever its filters. A ratings-only flag would
  spare it a watchlist write, but would be the cache's one per-write rule, to save one
  refetch after a bookmark.

### Touch

- **Tap-to-open on plain-text triggers only**, once in `Tooltip.svelte`. A control's tap
  stays its action — a bubble left over it would hide what the action did. Popover lost:
  no hover on desktop.
- **On `pointerup` in the capture phase**, not `click`: a scroll cancels it, iOS may send
  no click to a plain span, and capture leaves bits-ui's own handlers in place. No
  `preventDefault`, so a tooltip inside a card link still navigates. Accepted: on desktop
  a click no longer dismisses such a tooltip; moving away does.
- **Chip definitions are an ⓘ** (`InfoTip`) beside the group header. A key under each
  group lost — the Status key ran ~9 lines at desktop width.

### Genre groups

Users asked for "(Fantasy OR Isekai) AND NOT Drama".

- **One level of groups** — "any/all of these, without those" — joined by one switch.
  That is complete: any expression rewrites as an OR of AND-groups. Deeper nesting buys
  notation and costs a UI layer. The cap is 3 groups × 5 genres per list; what overflows
  is the rewrite blow-up (`((Action or Adventure) and (Fantasy or Sci-Fi)) or Mecha` is 5
  groups).
- **OR by default, the AND switch kept**: "one from each family" — (Shounen or Seinen)
  AND (Fantasy or Sci-Fi) — is 2 groups under AND and 4 under OR. One switch, not mixed
  connectors: mixing needs a precedence the cards can't show.
- **Warn, never prune** — the user's rule: never drop what someone built. A contradiction,
  a tautology or a group that changes nothing runs as built with a warning; simplified,
  it would return the same rows and only make blocks vanish. Only an empty group drops.
  `checkGenres` is exact because the expression is monotone per genre, so only genres
  named both ways need enumerating; past 16 of those it skips rather than warn wrongly.
- **NOT is the complement of the majority rule** over all media, so "Ecchi" and "without
  Ecchi" split the catalogue. "No media carries it" lost: a card without the badge would
  vanish from the results.
- **Old tokens fold, no version bump**: a token's `genre_name` + `genre_mode` become one
  group in a `before` validator, because tokens never expire.
- **One SQL pass over a genre array per row**, not a correlated subquery per genre, which
  grows superlinearly and fires on every edit of the field. Breaking the outer join fails
  exactly the Without-only tests.
- **The reads line mirrors the cards.** Rewriting it as alternatives lost: 3 AND-joined
  groups of 5 is 125 alternatives, ~4,000 characters.

### Discover exclusions

Studied on 2026-10-28 against the live `/search/anime`, each row with its default toggles,
against v0.16.3's bar of at most 5 shared first-25 tiles within a mood. A random row's
first 25 is one day's draw, so the expected share comes from the pools' overlap. Pool
sizes are all / Top 75% / unrated / unrated + Top 75%.

- **Unsolved** broke the bar twice: 7 shared with The detective's desk and 7 with Five
  steps ahead (5.4 expected from either). Without Detective alone left 11 shared with
  Five steps ahead; without Detective and Psychological takes both to 0, for
  113/87/102/76 → 76/55/67/46.
- **On stage** without Idols: 5 shared with Idol life → 0, for 77/56/72/51 → 52/40/47/35.
- **Beyond explanation** without Horror, Urban Fantasy, Vampire: shared with Lights off
  3 → 0, Haunting 4 → 3, Fangs at midnight 2 → 0, for 156/120/137/101 → 131/102/114/85.
- **Among the stars** without Mecha, which had kept it out of Future Shock with 9 shared:
  now 0 with every Future Shock row, for 33/25/32/24 → 20/14/20/14.
- **Just another day** (new, Cozy Corner): Slice of Life without Iyashikei and CGDCT,
  74/28/69/23, at most 3 shared with any Cozy row.
- **Not taken**: Beyond This World without Reincarnation / Villainess — 2 shared, and it
  cut a third of the biggest Other Worlds pool (162 → 103). No high-schoolers without
  Workplace — offices are what an adult cast looks like. Tournament arc without the
  sports — its noise (Gundam, Girls & Panzer, Conan) carries none of them.

## Failed approaches

- **The genre "+N" counted what wrapped out of the row.** The "+N" itself narrowed the
  row, so a card that widened stayed on "+1" and the observer re-fired on its own resize.
  It reuses `fitChipCount` instead.
- **`hugLines` on a ResizeObserver.** The chip's parent resizes with it, so every fit
  triggered another. It re-fits on window resize and text mutation.
- **TagSelect closing on a 150ms blur timer** shut the list right after a click reopened
  it. It closes on `focusout` leaving the whole picker.

## Gotchas & learnings

- **bits-ui 2.16.5's focus scope jumps.** When the focused node leaves, it refocuses the
  first tabbable with a bare `.focus()`, scrolling a sheet to the top. 2.20.x passes
  `preventScroll`; the handoff before removal stays for keyboard users
  (`rules/frontend.md`). 2.20 also unmounts closed content a frame late, after exit
  animations — a test asserting an immediate unmount needs `vi.waitFor`.
- **A lifted finger leaves its element.** A touch pointer gets `pointerleave` on lift;
  bits-ui's `SafePolygon` reads it as the pointer heading away and closes a frame later.
  The first tap survived because the bubble mounted after the leave. The span stops
  non-mouse `pointerleave` in the capture phase.
- **Tailwind 4's `space-y` is a zero-specificity `:where()`**: a child's margin beats it
  only while the child stands alone; in a row the row takes the parent's spacing on top.
  `BackLink` owns its row for this reason.
- **CSS sizes a box with wrapped text to the width available**, never its longest line —
  hence `hugLines`, and why a wrapped pill keeps its column's full width.
- **An inline margin applies at the start of a wrapped line**: a relation label's `ml`
  indented it on its own line, so the gap moved to the title link's `mr`.
- **`--bits-select-content-available-width` is not the room left of the anchor**: with
  `shift` on, floating-ui's `size` reports the viewport minus both paddings. A numeric
  `collisionPadding` also pads top and bottom, flipping a menu upward sooner.
- **The `Badge` primitive's `overflow-hidden` clips a hit area** — the TagSelect ✕'s
  32px target came out at 24px until that badge took `overflow-visible`.
- **jsdom**: no `matchMedia` (`tests/setup.ts` stubs it never-matching, so tests render
  the phone form) and no layout, so bits-ui finds no tabbable — focus-on-open can't be
  asserted.
