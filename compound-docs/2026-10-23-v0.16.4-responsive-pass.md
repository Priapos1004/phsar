# v0.16.4 — responsive pass (running notes)

## How the pass runs

The procedure became the `/ui-pass` skill: the user finds the issues live, Claude sets
the width and fixes what is agreed; 1280 was dropped from the widths because content
only changes at 1024 (search's two columns) and at the 2xl widening.

## /search — decisions

- **Gutter**: `main` was `px-8` at every width plus the container's `px-4` — 48px a
  side on a phone, leaving the card's text column 135px at 375. Now `px-4 sm:px-8`.
- **`auto-rows-fr` dropped** from `cls.mediaInfoGrid`: it sized every row to the
  grid's tallest card (measured: every card 720px, content 300–490px). Cards sharing a
  two-column row still match through grid stretch, and the card's content now fills
  the stretched card (`flex-1`) so the time/score row sits level with its neighbour's.
- **Phone card (< sm)**: cover 80×120 (was 96×144), 12px gap and padding — at 320 the
  column was 112px, narrower than "(2.3M ratings/media)" (~134px). The anime grain's
  relation/media-type count badges are hidden (the anime page has them); the media
  grain's single badges and the genres move to full-width rows under the cover.
  Rejected: an expandable card — the card is one link, a toggle can't sit inside it.
- **Genre line**: whole genres only, then "+N" — a ResizeObserver counts what wrapped
  out of a one-line row. Genres now order most-frequent-first across the anime's
  media (`most_frequent_first`, as studios and sources already did), so the line leads
  with the franchise's own; A→Z on a tie, and the media grain stays A→Z.
- **Season / status / age**: own lines on a phone (season, status, age); one line from
  sm (season, age, "(status)"). Season ranges are non-breaking inside each season and
  before the dash, so a wrap lands after the dash — in `formatSeasonRange`, so the
  share card gets it too.
- **Title + Complete badge**: plain flow, not `inline-flex` — the flex row squeezed the
  title to a word per line beside the badge.
- **Score + watch time**: compact counts ("1.2M") at every width; emoji replaced by
  lucide Star/Clock in `cls.glassIcon` (theme-colour outline, 25% tinted fill).
- **Description placeholder**: "Describe a story..." below 640px, via Svelte's
  `MediaQuery` (a placeholder is text CSS can't swap). jsdom lacks `matchMedia`, so
  `tests/setup.ts` stubs it never-matching — tests render the phone form.
- **Sort menu**: anchored to the trigger + arrow pair with `align="end"`, so its right
  edge sits under the arrow; capped at `100vw - 4rem`, which on a phone is exactly the
  content column. Shared with the see-all pages.
- **SpoilerGuard**: its wrapper is `h-fit` — a stretching flex parent made the overlay
  centre below the cover's middle. Label "Reveal" (was "Click to reveal": two lines on
  the phone cover, and "click" on a touchscreen).

## See-all pages (`/search/all`, `/browse/[key]`) — decisions

- **Top toggle on a phone**: six options overflow 320 at natural width; tighter padding
  alone doesn't rescue it. A 2×3 grid was offered and rejected — it breaks a percentage
  scale over two lines. `stretchOnPhone` on `PillToggle` / `SegmentedControl` makes the
  options share the row's width equally below sm. Beside a label that still overflowed
  the see-all bar (14px pills, ~35px per option), so there each group stacks its label
  over a full-width toggle; the sheet (12px) keeps the label beside.
- **Bar from sm**: `justify-between`, so a full line's last group sits under "Open in
  search"; where all groups fit one line (1024+) they spread across it.
- **"Open in search"** moved to the back link's line, styled `cls.pageNavLink` like the
  media page's "To anime". That surfaced the gap bug: a margin on the link (`mb-2`)
  overrides a parent's `space-y` only while the link stands alone (Tailwind 4's space-y
  is a zero-specificity `:where()`); in a row the row takes the parent's spacing on top.
  `BackLink` now owns the row and its `mb-2`, a right-hand link comes in as `children`,
  and the media page's `mb-0` workaround is gone.
- **Picker (TagSelect) in the sheet**: picking a genre scrolled the sheet to the top —
  the option button took focus on mousedown, was removed from the list once picked, and
  bits-ui's focus scope refocused the sheet's first element. `preventDefault` on the
  option's mousedown keeps focus in the input (the list now stays open after a pick).
  That exposed a second bug: closing on a 150ms blur timer shut the list right after a
  click on the box reopened it; it now closes on `focusout` leaving the whole picker.
  Mobile taps never hit either path.
- **Everything chosen = no filter**: `normalizeRated` generalised to `normalizeFilters`,
  dropping any "any of" list holding every offered option; "all" mode still filters.
- **Chips**: the category bold in the surface's own secondary shade — `white/70` on the
  dark search page, `card-foreground/70` on the light see-all card; `rounded-2xl` so a
  wrapped chip is a rounded rectangle (one-line chips look unchanged, the radius caps).
- **Navbar at 320**: the bell hides below 360px (343px needed with it) — by CSS, so its
  polling, and the job signals it feeds, stay live. (Superseded: "The bell at every
  width" below.)
- **Glass icon** now sized in em (`1.15em`) and filled from `currentColor`, so the tile's
  11px line and a banded tile's text colour carry it.

## Home and Browse — decisions

- **Season pill** (`cls.seasonPill`) never breaks its own text; a row header wraps the
  whole pill below the title instead ("Coming next season" at 320).
- **Hero height**: the 3:2 art (1536×1024) showed ~48% of its height in the 1024 banner
  but ~38% in the 1280 one, at the same 320px; `2xl:h-100` restores ~48%, the framing
  its focal points were tuned at.
- **Theme on login**: the login page navigates as soon as it sets the token, while the
  layout fetched settings in the background — the first page rendered in the default
  theme and art, then swapped, downloading two pictures. A fresh load already held the
  loading screen for the settings; a sign-in or user switch now does too. (A reload
  measured clean: settings, then one picture.)
- **Pictures**: the hero PNGs were 2–2.8 MB each (19 MB for eight), and the settings
  page loaded all four at full size for 224px tiles. WebP q85 heroes are 85–356 KB
  (1.6 MB total), 448px thumbnails 9–49 KB. The PNG originals moved to
  `frontend/art/` (dockerignored) for future edits; a test fails when a theme × season
  combination lacks its WebP or thumbnail.

## /anime and /media — decisions

- **Below 360px only** (`max-xs:` — a named `--breakpoint-xs: 22.5rem` in `app.css`, so the
  sub-360 tweaks move together; NavBar's bell uses it too): page gutters halve (`main` and `cls.container`
  px-4 → px-2) and the hero padding drops to p-4. At 320 the detail card's content was
  224px (hero 208px) — the cause of most 320 findings; 375+ untouched.
- **Score row**: one shared `HeroScore.svelte` for both heroes — wraps, the pill and the
  count never break inside, glass star (the carry-forward, now done).
- **Stats grid**: 4 columns only from lg (at md the hero's cover sits beside it, ~85px a
  column); "/ ep" instead of "per ep" at every width.
- **Attribute Summary**: radar + pill orbit side by side only from lg (the orbit's pills
  overflow a 314px half at md); the radar wraps its axis names below ~300px chart width.
- **Attribute selects**: one `AttributeGrid.svelte` for the rating form and the bulk
  dialog (the grid was duplicated — a fix landed in one copy only). Columns from the
  grid's own width, `max(9.5rem, half)` → one column on phones, never more than two.
  Once set, the ✕ takes the chevron's place (beside it, the two starved the value).
- **Rating summary**: phone — title, then the actions on one line (icons drop below
  360px); score + status in a row, pills full width beneath (a wrapped pill keeps its
  column's full width — CSS can't shrink a wrapped box to its longest line). 80px score
  from sm. Pill label and value are one inline span (as two badge flex items they sat
  side by side).
- **Attribute bars**: colour = the value's place in its option list (counting values
  present made "Rare" change colour). One fixed `ATTRIBUTE_COLOURS` set (rose, orange,
  emerald, blue) for every attribute — reads worst→best on quality attributes; a
  single-hue ramp was rejected earlier as too close, and two greens side by side
  (theme palette + scale) confused. Validated with the dataviz palette script against
  the real track (`bg-muted`, #e5e7eb) and the card: all pass; green↔orange ΔE 7.9 (protan)
  sits in the 6–8 band and orange is 2.26:1 on the track, both legal only with the text
  legend and the 2px segment gaps — keep both.
- **Floating surfaces**: 8px `collisionPadding` default in tooltip, dropdown (+sub),
  popover and select content; tooltip max width capped to the viewport; chart tooltips
  wrap inside their chart (`chartTooltipStyle`: `white-space: normal; max-width: 100%`).
- **Focus-trap jump** (now `rules/frontend.md`, "A control that removes itself hands
  focus on first"): an audit found it in TagSelect's chip ✕, the sheet's Clear all,
  BulkRateDialog's similar-titles retry and ShareDialog's retry; all fixed, the two
  sheet cases and AttributeSelect's ✕ tested. **Root cause is bits-ui**: 2.16.5's focus
  scope refocuses the first tabbable with a bare `.focus()` when the focused node leaves
  (a MutationObserver in `focus-scope.svelte.js`); 2.20.x passes `preventScroll: true`
  there, so the visual jump goes away for every dialog on upgrade (`package.json` already
  allows it — lockfile only). The handoffs stay worth having after that: without them a
  keyboard user lands on the dialog's first element. No hook exists to override the
  fallback in 2.16.5. Bumped to 2.20.2 with /ratings + /watchlist (`package.json` floor raised to
  match; floating-ui and tabbable moved with it). 2.20 also unmounts closed content a frame
  later, after any exit animation (`AnimationsComplete` waits a rAF, then on
  `getAnimations()`), so two tests asserting an immediate unmount now `vi.waitFor` it.
- **Bulk-select bar**: actions left-aligned after the count at every width (CSS can't
  right-align them only while they fit); media list titles clamp to two lines.

## /ratings and /watchlist — decisions

- **One quality scale** (`QUALITY_COLORS`): scores (`scoreColor`) and attribute bars share
  the histogram's red, yellow, green, purple — the user's pick, purple being the default
  theme's colour. Rejected: rose/orange/emerald/blue for both (the attribute set from the
  detail-page block), and a purple top with orange. The histogram set "fails" the palette
  script's lightness band only by yellow's L 0.772 against 0.77; CVD worst pair
  green↔yellow ΔE 8.4 passes; yellow 1.72:1 and green 2.0:1 on the `bg-muted` track, so
  marks need labels. As text colour (the table's score, the band numbers) yellow is
  2.1:1 on white; kept — one scale for marks and text (rules/frontend.md).
- **Ratings table**: every column at every width — on a phone it scrolls sideways in its
  card (528px in 302 at 320) instead of hiding MAL and Rated, whose sorts were out of reach
  without rotating the phone. Title cell `min-w-36` (unset, a squeezed title breaks after
  every word; `min-width` on a `td` holds in Chrome). Relation label `whitespace-nowrap`;
  its gap is the title link's `mr-1.5`, not the label's `ml-1.5` — an inline margin
  still applies at the start of a wrapped line, so the label sat indented. A sticky title
  column was rejected: the card is translucent glass, so a sticky cell needs an opaque
  background.
- **Overview watch time**: `formatDurationCompact` ("122d 8h"), the watchlist Queued time
  tile's format — to the second it wrapped and threw the 2×2 tiles out of line.
- **Categories**: label column fixed `w-24 sm:w-36`. Content-sized was tried first (it
  freed the bars on short genre names) but the chart jumped whenever the direction flip
  swapped the names; 6rem on a phone because 9rem of studio names left the chart ~120px.
  Sideways-scrolling the chart was rejected: it hides the bar ends the comparison lives
  in. Axis ticks `hideOverlap`. Dimension toggle `stretchOnPhone` in a `max-sm:w-full`
  wrapper (without it, `flex-1` squeezes the toggle in beside the heading); the sort
  select fills its row on a phone so the arrow stays beside it.
- **Attributes**: every chart on the tab shares one label column (`containLabel: false`,
  `axisLabel.width` + truncate) so the axes start at the same x — 96px from sm (full
  names), 72px on a phone with the quality scales by first word. Read via
  `MediaQuery('min-width: 640px')`, the SearchBar precedent.
- **Activity**: heading and toggle `flex-wrap` — the toggle drops under the heading when the
  two don't fit (a phone) instead of squeezing it. `SegmentedControl` options never wrap.
- **Ratings/watchlist view row**: Grid/Table drop their icons below 360px (`max-xs:`).
- **Watchlist**: table shows Added at every width and scrolls (as ratings). Overview tiles
  reworked into one card like the ratings Overview (figure over label, small accent icon;
  2 columns below md, the fifth centred) — the 44px icon chips left ~77px for "105d 19h"
  (111px) and "CONTINUATIONS" (103px). Lists tab rows are at most two lines: name truncates,
  count below it on a phone — wrapped, a 50-char name ran 2–5 lines (row ~191px at 375,
  ~136px at 320; counts 119–139px). Keeping the 50-char limit: lowering it can't remove the
  need, ~25 wide chars already overflow a 320 row.
- **Long text, as a class** (a user's string up to 50 chars without a space): `body
  { overflow-wrap: break-word }` breaks it wherever it sits in running text (dialog titles,
  toasts, notes, the bell's error) — chosen over per-site `wrap-anywhere`, which each author
  had to remember. break-word doesn't lower min-content, so text that is a flex item of its
  own keeps `wrap-anywhere` (the removal toggle's label, the see-all title, tooltips);
  `anywhere` globally was rejected because it does lower min-content and would collapse
  auto-sized table columns. Controls truncate (list chips, the list picker and its options,
  search-sheet chips). Fixed once in the primitives: `Dialog.Content` gets
  `grid-cols-1` (minmax(0,1fr) — an implicit auto column was floored by the widest child's
  min-content, the root of every per-dialog `min-w-0`, now removed); select lists cap at
  `--bits-select-content-available-width`; `Dialog.Title` gets `leading-snug pe-6` (a
  wrapped title crowded and ran under the ✕); tooltips `wrap-anywhere`. The toast was
  centred by `left-1/2` with no width, so it sized to the right half of the screen (~150px).

## /settings and /library/add — decisions

- **Preference rows** stack below sm (`cls.stackOnPhone`, select full width): side by side
  with no gap, the spoiler description touched its select (0px at 320 and 375) and the
  selects squeezed to 110–160px, a ragged column. From sm: `gap-4`, selects `shrink-0` at
  160px. `cls.stackOnPhone` now stretches a fixed-width control too (`max-sm:*:w-full`) —
  its `items-stretch` never beat a `w-40`.
- **One width for the user-menu pages**: `/settings` widened 2xl → 3xl to match
  `/library/add` and `/admin`; `/library/add` took the section-page shell (white h1,
  `space-y-6`, no `py-12`, the form card without its own title), so heading and first card
  land in the same place across the three. The three share `cls.panelWidth`, as anime
  pages share `cls.pageWidth` — Settings had drifted as a literal.
- **Placeholder** "e.g. Naruto, Attack on Titan" (198px) — the three-title one was 291px in
  the 250px a 320 input has. One string at every width; a placeholder can't switch by
  breakpoint without script.
- **The bell at every width**: below 360px the bar takes
  the page's own `px-2` and 8px gaps, which fits the bell at 320 with 17px to spare — rather
  than a per-page job list wherever the bell is missing, which every page that starts a job
  would have needed. The enqueue toast drops "Track progress in the bell"; the page's own
  paragraph says it.
- **Bell panel**: `w-80` overflowed 320 (304px of room inside the 8px collision padding), so
  the dropdown primitive caps at `--bits-dropdown-menu-content-available-width`, as the
  select lists do. Where it fits (~410px up) `alignOffset={8}` ends it under the bell glyph
  rather than the button's invisible 36px circle.
- **Bell focus ring**: bits-ui focuses the first tabbable on open, and the rows are plain
  links (not menu items), so the first finished row took a theme-coloured ring when the
  browser judged the focus keyboard focus. The panel takes focus instead. jsdom finds no
  tabbable (no layout), so no test can separate the two.
- **Carousel end tiles**: a ring, outline, hover scale or the return glow draws outside the
  tile and was cut at the row's first and last tile, and at the top where a row had no
  `pt-1`. `ScrollRow` owns the track's padding (`px-1 pt-1 pb-2`; callers pass only their
  gap), the scroller reaches out sideways by the same (`-mx-1`, `scroll-px-1` for snapping); BrowseCarousel's
  `.row-body` clips vertically only — its `overflow: clip` on both axes was the second cut.

## /search at 1920 — the width decision

Content capped at 1024 left the cards in about half of a 1920 screen. Three search
columns were rejected: the list is ranked (a wider zigzag loses the order), the card is
text-dense and reads best ~450–650px wide (three would make it ~405), and results get
compared side by side. Image-first browsing already has many-column poster tiles
(Browse, the see-all pages). So two columns stay, and every anime page widens to 7xl
from 2xl together, keeping the shared edges — search cards 488 → 616px, fewer badge rows.

## Gotchas

- bits-ui's `--bits-select-content-available-width` is not "the room left of the
  anchor": bits-ui turns on `shift` on the x axis, and with it floating-ui's `size`
  reports the viewport minus both paddings — the same as `calc(100vw - 4rem)`. And a
  numeric `collisionPadding` pads top and bottom too, shrinking the menu's height and
  flipping it upward sooner. The sort menu keeps the literal cap.
- The genre "+N" reuses `fitChipCount` (`shareContent.ts`). A first version counted the
  items that wrapped out of the row, but the "+N" itself narrowed that row, so a card
  that widened stayed on "+1" and the observer re-fired on its own resize.

- dev-browser: a viewport taller than the screen's usable height (1024 here) pushes
  the page bottom off-monitor — it looks like the page can't scroll to the end. Use
  ~900px heights.
